StreamLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1st October 2026

This policy explains what information the StreamLink channel for Roku devices ("StreamLink", "the app", "we", "us") uses and how it is handled. StreamLink is operated by Maniverse. You can contact us at toyear2023@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • StreamLink has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The video links you paste are sent to our service to create a stream link.
  • Your saved videos and daily usage counters are stored on your Roku device, not on our servers.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Roku. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not show ads and we do not sell your information.

2. Information we handle

Video links you submit. When you press Stream, or open a saved video whose link has expired, the link you pasted is sent to our service and to a third-party API provider that returns a playable stream link, together with details such as the title, author, thumbnail and duration. We use this only to give you the stream link.

Device identifier. Our service may receive an anonymous identifier that Roku provides to channels, together with the request. We use it only to prevent abuse and to apply fair-use limits. It does not identify you personally.

Technical information. Like any internet service, the servers that handle a request can see technical details such as the IP address and the time of the request. We use this only to operate and protect the service.

Information stored on your device. StreamLink stores on your Roku: the videos you saved (link, title, author, thumbnail, stream link), your daily usage counters for the free plan, and the subscription state. This information stays on your device until you remove it with "Clear history" in Settings, or remove the channel.

3. Subscriptions and payments

StreamLink Pro is sold through Roku Pay. Roku processes the payment and manages billing, renewal and cancellation. We do not receive your card or billing details. Roku tells the app whether your subscription is active so we can remove the daily limits.

4. Third parties

  • Roku provides the device platform and payments, under its own privacy policy.
  • API and hosting providers. We use a third-party API provider to create stream links and, if used, a hosting provider to relay requests. They process the links you submit to deliver the service.
  • Video services. When you play a stream, your Roku connects to the servers of the video service that hosts the video, and that service can see your IP address and viewing request. StreamLink is not affiliated with those services.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not build a profile of you or your viewing.

6. Retention

Saved videos and usage counters are kept on your device until you delete them. Our service does not keep a history of the links you submit beyond the short time needed to process a request, apart from routine technical logs kept by our providers for security and operation.

7. Children

StreamLink is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

You can clear saved videos at any time using "Clear history" in Settings, or by removing the channel from your Roku. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we do not collect personal information such as your name or email, there is usually nothing to look up, but you can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with any request or question.

9. Security

Requests between the app and our service use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we handle.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
